Oh No My Image

Interactive visual prototype

Turn a photo into living particles.

Upload an image, leave it untouched in stillness, then break it apart with your cursor. Hold the pointer down to turn that point into a slow attractor while roaming enemies chew through pixels on contact.

Move through the pixels to hit them. Hold to pull them off-course and dodge the enemies.

Awaiting an upload.

Desktop-first. Best with a photo that has strong contrast.
